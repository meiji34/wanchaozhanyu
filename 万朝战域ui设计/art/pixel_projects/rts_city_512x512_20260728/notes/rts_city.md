# rts_city

- Category: backgrounds
- Size: 512x512
- Colors used: locked subset of Three Kingdoms Fortress 48
- Animation: none
- View: three-quarter isometric top-down
- Design notes: A complete fortified Three Kingdoms-era RTS city with a square defensive silhouette, four corner watchtowers, a prominent southern gatehouse, central palace compound, barracks, warehouses, courtyards and readable internal roads. Red lacquer roofs establish hierarchy; blue-green roofs and muted vegetation prevent the warm palette from becoming visually flat. Top-left lighting and dark outlines preserve readability at reduced map scale.
- Variants: none
- Godot import: use nearest-neighbor texture filtering and preserve alpha
