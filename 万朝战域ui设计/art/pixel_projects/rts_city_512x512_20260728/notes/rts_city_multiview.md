# rts_city_multiview

- Category: backgrounds / modeling reference
- Master sheet size: 1536x1024
- Cell size: 512x512
- Views: front, right, rear, left, top, three-quarter isometric
- Colors used: locked subset of Three Kingdoms Fortress 48
- Animation: none
- Design notes: The sheet preserves the original city's square wall footprint, four corner watchtowers, southern gatehouse, central palace and secondary compounds. Elevation views share a consistent baseline and visual scale. The top and isometric views expose the internal road and building layout for blockout modeling.
- Modeling note: Use the front/side views for height proportions and the top view for footprint placement. Because these are AI-generated concept references, reconcile small roof and flag differences during the 3D blockout rather than treating every ornament as measured construction data.
